Unbroken RN — Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026.

The short version: Unbroken RN collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. Everything you record stays in the app's storage on your own device.

What the app stores, and where

Your entries (break records, schedule, pay settings, and the app's sealed record of them) are stored only on your device. The developer has no copy, no access, and no way to recover them for you.

Network use

The app makes exactly one kind of network request: a clock-accuracy check against Apple's public time server (a standard 48-byte time request to time.apple.com). It carries no personal information and none of your entries — it only lets the app note how closely your device clock matched a public time server. The app works fully offline; when offline, entries simply record the clock check as unavailable.

How data leaves the device

Only when you export it. PDF, spreadsheet, and backup files are created on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet; you choose where they go. Backup files are encrypted with a passphrase you choose — the developer cannot read or unlock them.

Apple Watch

If you use the watch app, entries travel from your watch to your paired iPhone over Apple's device-to-device connection. No third-party server is involved.

Notifications

All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push service and no server sending you anything.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Erase all data on this phone" removes every entry and setting, or you can simply delete the app. Either way, nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever anywhere else.

Selling or sharing data

Nothing is collected, so there is nothing to sell or share. The app contains no third-party code that could collect data on its own.

Children

Unbroken RN is a workplace documentation tool for working nurses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because the app has no way to contact you, check this page after major app updates.
