Unbroken RN — Support

Unbroken RN is a charting tool for nurses working under automatic meal-break deductions: one tap per shift builds a dated, sealed, tamper-evident record of missed and interrupted breaks, entirely on your own phone.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone only. There is no account and no server. Export a passphrase-encrypted backup regularly (Settings → Backup) — if the phone is lost, the record is lost with it unless you have a backup file.

Why does a figure show $0? Usually one of two honest reasons: no base pay rate has been entered yet (Settings → Pay), or the week stayed under the overtime threshold — the app labels that plainly rather than inflating a number.

Why is an entry marked LATE ENTRY? Entries recorded well after a shift are labeled, the same way late charting is labeled at work. The label is part of the record's honesty.

The in-app Help (Settings → How to use this app) answers the rest — capture modes, the sealed record, exports, and backups.
